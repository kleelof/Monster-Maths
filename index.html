HOW TO PLAY

Clear the monsters with maths

  1. Hold the mouse button to move toward the pointer without letting a monster touch you.
  2. Click a monster to see its question, then type the answer and press Enter.
  3. Some monsters are faster and more difficult to destroy than others.
  4. Correct answers clear monsters. Wrong answers let you try again without losing a life.
  5. Hold Space while mouse-moving to teleport freely. Teleporting drains the energy meter.
  6. Defeating a monster restores a little teleport energy; it refills fully between waves.
  7. Clear three waves to reach the next maths level.
